SupoClip Personal - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information SupoClip Personal ("the App") handles and how it is used. The App is a personal, self-hosted tool used to create short video clips and publish them to the owner's own social media accounts.

1. Information we handle

  • Videos and clips you provide. Videos you upload or link, and the clips generated from them, are processed and stored on the server where the App runs.
  • Account information. The email address and name you use to create an account in the App.
  • TikTok account information. When you connect TikTok, the App receives, through TikTok's official API and only with your permission, your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, and the public information about your videos (such as video IDs, view, like, comment and share counts).
  • Access tokens. The authorization tokens TikTok provides so the App can publish on your behalf. They are stored in the App's own database.

2. How we use the information

  • To process your videos and generate clips.
  • To publish or schedule clips to your TikTok account when you ask the App to do so.
  • To show you the performance of the clips you published.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Third-party services

To provide its features, the App may send data to these services:

  • TikTok, to log you in and publish your clips.
  • AssemblyAI, which receives the audio of your videos to create a transcript.
  • Google (Gemini API), which receives the transcript text to select the best moments of your video.

Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

4. Storage and security

Your data is stored on the server or computer where the App is installed. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no system is completely secure.

5. Retention and deletion

You can delete your videos, clips and tasks inside the App at any time. You can disconnect your TikTok account in the App, which removes the stored tokens, and you can also revoke the App's access from your TikTok account settings. To request deletion of your account and data, contact us at the email below.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
